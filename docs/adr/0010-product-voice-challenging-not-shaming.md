# Product voice: challenging, never shaming

Affordo's voice is deliberately provocative in service of its mission —
encouraging people to save and to avoid impulse debt (loans/BNPL for an iPhone),
in the spirit of *Your Money or Your Life*. It makes cost tangible and actively
**challenges the user to think twice** about significant purchases.

The guardrail is a hard invariant: **challenge the decision, never demean the
person.** Acceptable: "This is 12 work days of your life — worth pausing on?"
Not acceptable: calling the user irresponsible/stupid, implying "you're living
beyond your means," or any shame aimed at the person. Especially in the
Not Reachable case, stay constructive (levers they control), never scolding.

The challenge is triggered by a **Significance Threshold** (a share of income,
user-customizable) above which the app prompts reflection. No dark patterns —
the nudge always points toward saving/reflection, never toward spending.

This voice is product-wide; do not soften it into a neutral calculator, and do
not let it drift into judgment of the person.
